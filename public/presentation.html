The Problem

Why Canteens Bleed Food & Money

Institutional food managers lack an operational telemetry loop. By the time overproduction is noticed, it's already in the trash bin.

1. The Gut-Instinct Trap

Head chefs prepare fixed quantities regardless of calendar seasonality, exam periods, or hostel departure cycles, causing systematic overproduction on specific weekdays.

2. Incompatible Units

Rice and curries are weighed in kg, while snack plates and idlis are counted in portions. Existing tools mix these or force inaccurate conversions that distort analytics.

3. Dead Redistribution

Usable surplus has a strict 2-hour safety window. Organizers have no structured, time-sensitive system to notify student welfare teams or local food banks before food expires.

The Solution

The PlateWise Operational Loop

A full-stack intelligence platform that records production, isolates recurring waste patterns, forecasts upcoming demand, and routes usable surplus.

Log with Unit Segregation

Strict segregation between kilograms and meal counts. Live arithmetic validation guarantees Served + Remaining = Prepared with zero impossible totals.

Detect Recurring Imbalances

Automated pattern alarms flag recipes that repeatedly exceed 15% waste threshold (e.g. Chicken Biryani at Lunch detected 11×).

Forecast & Rescue

Predictive preparation targets calculated via recency decay and day seasonality, paired with an emergency surplus dispatch hub.
